The Olive Estate
wedding photographer

The Olive Estate in Henley Brook is an amazing rural-style property filled with lots of great spots for photos. The venue offers ceremony, reception and accommodation all in one.

That last part matters more than couples expect. Because everything happens on one property, nobody loses forty minutes to travel — which means more time for photos, and a day that doesn't feel rushed.

We shot Tayla & Brodie here under the trees, with the light coming through in patches. Dappled light is difficult and it's also where the best frames come from if you know how to meter for it.
