Wedding photo booth
hire in Perth

The booth is the thing your guests will queue for. Somewhere between the speeches and the last song it becomes the loudest corner of the room — and you end up with a gallery of everyone you love pulling faces.

It's an open-air booth, which matters more than it sounds. There's no curtain and no cramped box — just a backdrop and proper studio lighting, so eight people can pile into one frame instead of two.

An attendant runs it all night, so nobody in your wedding party ends up troubleshooting a printer during the reception. Props and backdrops come with it, there's no cap on how many photos get taken, and everything lands in an online gallery afterwards.

Set-up and pack-down sit outside your service hours. A three-hour booking is three hours of working booth, not three hours minus however long it takes to build the thing.

If you're already booking photography with us, the booth is the simplest way to keep a reception going after dinner — and it's the same team, so there's one supplier to deal with rather than two.

Common questions

Good to know

How much is photo booth hire in Perth?

Our wedding photo booth packages start at $799 for a three hour service, $899 for four hours and $1,099 for six hours.

What's included with the photo booth?

The booth itself, an attendant to run it, props, backdrops, unlimited photos and an online gallery afterwards. Set-up and pack-down are separate to your service hours.

Is it an open-air booth?

Yes. It's an open-air booth with a backdrop and studio lighting rather than an enclosed box, so large groups fit into a single frame.
