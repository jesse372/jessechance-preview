Good to know

Questions couples ask us

We're not photogenic. At all.

Nobody thinks they are, and then we show them the gallery. You won't be posed into anything stiff — we mostly get you moving, talking and laughing at each other, then shoot what actually happens. The people who tell us they "hate photos" are usually the ones who end up loving theirs the most.

How long until we get our photos?

Six to eight weeks for your full gallery, with every frame hand-edited rather than run through a preset. You'll get a small set of sneak peeks well before that, so you've got something to share while the day still feels fresh.

How many photos do we actually get?

Unlimited selected images in high resolution — every good frame from the day, edited. We don't cap your gallery or charge per image.

What if it rains?

Then we shoot in the rain. Some of our favourite images have come out of terrible weather — one of our couples described us standing in a downpour keeping them relaxed. We'll always have a plan B for your location, and it's usually better than plan A.

Do you travel outside Perth?

Yes — Perth Hills, Swan Valley, the coast, the South West and further. Elopements especially. Tell us where you're headed and we'll sort the details.

Can we do photo, video and a booth together?

That's the easiest way to do it — one team, one timeline, nobody bumping into each other on the dance floor. Mention it in your enquiry and we'll put a combined quote together for you.
