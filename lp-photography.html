Is $799 a real price?
Yes. It is the Bronze collection at its current sale price, down from $1,299, and it includes unlimited hand-edited images with no per-image charges. There is no bait here — the same prices are on our main site. What changes between collections is hours of coverage, not whether you get your photos.
What does “unlimited images” actually mean?
Every frame worth keeping, edited by hand, delivered in high resolution. No cap on your gallery and no charge per image. A lot of photographers deliver fifty pictures and sell you the rest — we do not.
How quickly do we get them?
Six to eight weeks, with a full year of cloud storage included.
What if it rains?
It has rained on plenty of our weddings and the photos were still good — one of the reviews above is literally about it. Overcast light is softer on faces than hard summer sun. We plan a wet-weather option with you beforehand so nobody is improvising on the day.
What if you are already booked, or someone gets sick?
There are eight photographers on the team, so your date is rarely a problem, and if someone falls ill the week of your wedding there is a professional ready to step in. Most photographers cannot say that.
What happens after we enquire?
We reply within 24 hours and tell you straight away whether we are free. No pressure and no sales pitch — if we are not the right fit we will say so.

Tell us when and where. We’ll come back within 24 hours — no pressure, no sales pitch.
