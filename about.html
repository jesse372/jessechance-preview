About us

Your love,
your story

Knowing firsthand how special this moment is, and how nervous you may feel, our mission is to make you comfortable and be true to yourself.

Whether you are having an intimate wedding in a backyard or an elopement on a mountain top, we are ready with our cameras. All our focus is on you. It's all about raw emotions, having fun, and creating memories that will last a lifetime.

Why we love what we do

Hopeless romantics,
professionally

Weddings are truly one of the most special moments in a person's life. There is so much emotion all around and people seem to really put away their differences (for the most part) and come together to celebrate as a family.

Being surrounded by all that love and warmth is something we really enjoy. The way a mum's eyes mist up when she sees her son all grown up ready to start his own family. That instant when a dad realises that his little girl is not so little anymore. The first kiss between a married couple.

What can we say — we are hopeless romantics at heart and really enjoy being part of someone's special day. There is always something going on, and each wedding we photograph is different from the last.

You get to spend the day capturing the romance, laughter, joy, anticipation, excitement, and many other photographic moments.

Many hats

Every style at once

Candid shots, family portraits, environmental details, food, florals — often within the same ten minutes. We move between them without you noticing.
