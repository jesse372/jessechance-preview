Before you ask

The fine print, in plain English

What does "unlimited selected images" actually mean?

Every frame worth keeping from your coverage, edited and delivered in high resolution. We cull the blinks and the duplicates — you get everything else. There's no cap and no charge per image.

How do we secure our date?

A simple contract and a deposit. Once that's done the date is yours and we start planning the timeline together.

Can we customise a package?

Almost always. If you need six hours instead of five, or photo plus a booth plus a film, just tell us what your day looks like and we'll build the quote around it.

Do you cover elopements and small weddings?

Some of our favourite work is elopements — a backyard, a clifftop, the Perth Hills at sunrise. The Bronze and Silver packages suit these beautifully.

Is travel included?

Perth metro is included. For the South West, up north or interstate, we'll quote travel transparently up front so there are no surprises.
